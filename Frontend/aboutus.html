About LocalBiz Connect

A simple full stack learning project for beginners

Project Idea

LocalBiz Connect helps a small buisness display services online and collect enquiries from customers

Why We Are Building This

This project helps students understand how a real web project grows.HTML structure,CSS design,JavaScript interaction,backend,API,Database and final submission

Future Scope

In later days,the contact form can be connected to Flask backed and SQLLite database.Then the dashboard can show real enquiry data.
